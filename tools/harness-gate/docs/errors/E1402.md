## Why this fails

Verification was cancelled before successful completion. The cancellation source and cleanup outcome are both relevant to the next action.

## Diagnose and repair

Identify the cancellation source and confirm cleanup outcomes. Rerun the intended verification after cancellation is resolved; cancelled work is not successful evidence.

Use the accompanying error and retained logs to identify the failed operation.
A corrected invocation is not itself a passing verification; inspect the new
result after rerunning the intended command.

## Example

After a user interrupt or CI cancellation, inspect cleanup and retained ownership evidence before rerunning. Do not treat partially written reports as a completed verification, and do not delete uncertain resources solely by a reused name.
