## Why this fails

A reporting-stage operation failed. Allocation, report writing, quality publication, legacy-output mirroring and webhook notification can fail at this boundary. Notification runs after local publication, so local artifacts may already be complete even though the command failed.

## Diagnose and repair

Read the original error to identify the stage. For local output failures inspect storage, permissions and finalization. For notification failures inspect endpoint availability, delivery errors and egress policy. Preserve the command failure and inspect local artifact status rather than assuming every artifact is incomplete.

## Example

A denied webhook destination can produce E1404 after local reports have been published. Correct the intended approved endpoint or delivery configuration without bypassing egress or redirect restrictions. Do not rewrite completed reports or mark the failed command successful to hide a notification failure.
