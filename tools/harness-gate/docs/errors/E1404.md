## Why this fails

Verification could not finish writing its report. Successful individual steps alone do not establish a complete published verification result.

## Diagnose and repair

Check the report destination, free space, permissions and evidence finalization error. Preserve incomplete evidence for diagnosis and rerun after correcting the cause.

Use the accompanying error and retained logs to identify the failed operation.
A corrected invocation is not itself a passing verification; inspect the new
result after rerunning the intended command.

## Example

When report publication fails because storage is full, retain available diagnostics, restore capacity and rerun the required verification. Never manually mark an incomplete manifest as finalized or relabel an older report as current.
