## Why this fails

The requested profile or step cannot be selected under the workflow configuration. A step may exist but not belong to the selected profile.

## Diagnose and repair

Check the configured profile and step identifiers and whether the step belongs to the selected profile. Use verify --plan to inspect the corrected execution plan.

Use the accompanying error and retained logs to identify the failed operation.
A corrected invocation is not itself a passing verification; inspect the new
result after rerunning the intended command.

## Example

If selecting a single step, confirm its exact identifier and profile membership. Inspect verify --plan after correcting the selection. Selecting a different subset is not equivalent to running the originally required profile.
