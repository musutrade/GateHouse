# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Harness-Gate contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate with
respect, focus criticism on the work, welcome relevant technical questions,
and avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, or the
publication of another person's private information.

This standard applies in repository discussions, reviews, issue trackers,
release coordination, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not include credentials, customer data, or other
unrelated sensitive material. Maintainers will review reports impartially,
limit disclosure to people needed for resolution, and may edit or remove
content, issue a warning, restrict participation, or permanently exclude a
participant when necessary.

Anyone handling a report in which they have a conflict of interest must recuse
themselves. Retaliation against a reporter or participant in an investigation
is not permitted.
